Why Calculus?

No matter where you stand in the STEM world, from medicine to engineering, there's one central math you need: Calculus. Even though it is complicated, Calculus is common in daily life and in your own chosen STEM career, from predicting how bridges handle wind to seeing how fast a medication enters a bloodstream.

I'd recommend this as the one shared course between all STEM disciplines, as it is fundamental to understanding concepts like rate of change and the accumulation of area.

This course covers three main topics: limits, derivatives, and integrals. You will learn what they are and what they represent, along with how they relate to each other and how they are used in real life and in analysis.
