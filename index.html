How Intellecta Works

Intellecta combines the rigor and depth of LibreTexts with the interactivity and approachability of Khan Academy.

The structure

  1. Subject: a field of study, such as Mathematics.
  2. Course: a full semester-scale body of material, such as Calculus I.
  3. Unit: a coherent block inside a course, such as Derivatives.
  4. Lesson: one teachable idea, such as the Chain Rule.

Inside every lesson

Article
Choose your depth. The Intuition tab explains the idea in plain language with visuals and simulations, focused on getting you to understand the concept. The Standard tab adds definitions and worked examples, focused on having you beign able to apply the concept you have learnt with formulas, definitions, etc. The Rigorous tab adds precise statements, proofs, and derivations, for those who already know the topic but want to understand how exactly it works.
Exercises
Practice in four tiers: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and Challenge. Every problem gives instant feedback, a hint ladder, and a full worked solution.
Video
A short explainer of three to eight minutes. Videos are optional, and no lesson depends on one.

How mastery works

Every lesson is tied to named skills, and your progress is tracked skill by skill:

  • Not started: no attempts yet.
  • Attempted: you have tried the skill.
  • Familiar: you are getting recent basic and intermediate problems right.
  • Proficient: you are reliably getting recent problems right, including intermediate ones.
  • Mastered: you passed a spaced re-check at least a week later.

Each unit ends with a unit check. Each course ends with a mastery exam that covers recall, concepts, calculation, derivation, application, cross-disciplinary thinking, programming, and a challenge section. If you score below 80% on an exam, Intellecta shows you which skills to review so you can retest.

Three ways to learn

  • Pathway mode: follow a curated sequence of courses tailored to your chosen career.
  • Free browse: pick any course given by Intellecta, either for review, a class, etc.
  • Skill practice: drill a single skill whenever you want for practice and review.

Not sure where you fit? Take the placement diagnostic. It is a short quiz that recommends a starting course and flags skills to review first.

The Achievement System

Achievements recognize real progress, not time spent. Each one is earned from what you actually demonstrate, such as skill mastery, exam scores, and project rubrics, and each one records the date and the evidence behind it.

Course achievements

Every course has its own named achievement, earned by passing every unit and scoring at least 90% on the course mastery exam. For example, completing Electricity & Circuits earns the Faraday Achievement. Each course achievement is ranked by how hard the course is, so Python Foundations sits at a different rank than Astrodynamics. There are 64 named course achievements so far.

Why Intellecta?

Intellecta is a community-driven learning platform built to bridge the gap between introductory science and advanced university-level problem solving.

Everything is designed to be accessible: interactive lessons work with a keyboard, images have text descriptions, and videos come with captions and transcripts, tailored to every type of learning.

Every lesson carries a status of Draft, Reviewed, or Verified, so you always know how closely it has been checked. If you find a mistake, every page has a report-a-problem link, and anyone can contribute lessons, exercises, and corrections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Intellecta free?

Yes. Intellecta offers free, interactive lessons.

Do I have to follow a pathway?

No. You can follow a pathway, browse the Library freely, or practice a single skill. A pathway is just a suggested order for the courses.

What if I am not ready for a course?

Every course lists its prerequisites, and each lesson links back to the lessons it builds on. The placement diagnostic can also recommend a starting course.

Do I need to watch the videos?

No. Videos are optional, and every lesson can be completed without one.

Do achievements reward speed?

No. Achievements reward demonstrated skill, depth, and contribution, never speed or daily streaks.

Where is my progress saved?

By default, your progress is stored in your own browser. Accounts may be added later as an optional feature.
